# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)
The course opens.
- 18 holes: a front nine and a back nine, par 57, each with a silly name and at least one clever line.
- Slingshot putting with mouse or touch, a dotted preview to the first bounce, and full keyboard controls.
- Things to hit, dodge and ride: slanted walls, narrow gaps, sand, water, ice, slopes, conveyor belts, boost pads, bumpers, sliding blocks, windmills, pipes, a doorbell-operated door and a one-way gate.
- Full rounds with a scorecard and a medal, or practice on any unlocked hole. Unfinished rounds resume after a reload.
- Stars for every hole, best scores, a hole-in-one counter, and seven balls to unlock.
- Plays in landscape on desktop and turns the course sideways to fill a portrait phone.
