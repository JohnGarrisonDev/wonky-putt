# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-26)
Plays better on phones.
- Full screen on every phone, iPhone included: tap "Play full screen" under the game. Upright or sideways, the course re-fits to the screen.
- Cancel a putt: slide back to where you pressed (a bigger spot on touch screens) and let go, or touch with a second finger.
- The daily card, its "how was it?" vote and the final scorecard fit on small phones, with bigger vote buttons.
- The scorecard lays out properly again, and sits side by side on short landscape screens.
- A bigger menu button, the top bar clear of notches in full screen, and a small buzz on a putt, a splash and a sunk ball (Android).

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-25)
Tell us what you think.
- After today's official try, tell us how hard the daily hole was with one tap, and see how your strokes compare with everyone else's today. Anonymous: nothing about you is stored.
- A new **Tell us** card under the game: a thumbs up or down and a few one-tap notes (too hard, too easy, broke on my phone...). No account, no typing.
- A Privacy link in the footer explains exactly what is counted.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)
A new hole every day.
- **Daily hole**: one generated hole per day, the same for everyone, with a silly name to match. The robots build it from the parts you know (sand, water, ice, slopes, bumpers, windmills, pipes, boosters, sliding blocks and wonky walls), one to three at a time.
- Gentle on Mondays and Tuesdays, spicier by the weekend. Every daily is checked by the robots' own solver before it goes out: it can be finished within par, it is not a free hole in one, and the ball cannot get stuck.
- Your first try is the official one: every stroke counts until it drops (or par + 5). After that, practise it as much as you like; practice never changes your score.
- Day streaks, your best streak, a strip of the last seven days, and a Copy result button with a spoiler-free line to share.
- Today's daily and your streak show on the title screen. An unfinished official try picks up where you left it after a reload.
- Your course progress, stars and balls carry over untouched.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)
The course opens.
- 18 holes: a front nine and a back nine, par 57, each with a silly name and at least one clever line.
- Slingshot putting with mouse or touch, a dotted preview to the first bounce, and full keyboard controls.
- Things to hit, dodge and ride: slanted walls, narrow gaps, sand, water, ice, slopes, conveyor belts, boost pads, bumpers, sliding blocks, windmills, pipes, a doorbell-operated door and a one-way gate.
- Full rounds with a scorecard and a medal, or practice on any unlocked hole. Unfinished rounds resume after a reload.
- Stars for every hole, best scores, a hole-in-one counter, and seven balls to unlock.
- Plays in landscape on desktop and turns the course sideways to fill a portrait phone.
