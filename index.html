Wonky Putt

The robots built a golf course. Nothing on it is straight.

How to play

  • Get the ball in the cup in as few putts as you can. There are 18 holes, a front nine and a back nine.
  • Slow balls drop in. Fast balls hop over the cup, so ease off near the flag.
  • Sand eats speed, ice keeps it, water costs a stroke. Arrows push, boosters fling, bumpers boing, pipes swap places.
  • Under par earns three stars, par two, finishing one. Stars unlock new balls.
  • A hole ends at par plus five. The robots move you on, no hard feelings.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: press anywhere on the course, pull back like a slingshot, let go. Longer pull, harder putt.
  • Keyboard: ← → aim, ↑ ↓ power, Space or Enter to putt. Hold Shift for fine aim.
  • R restarts the hole (a mulligan in a full round). Esc opens the menu or cancels an aim.
  • The dotted line shows where the ball goes up to its first bounce.
